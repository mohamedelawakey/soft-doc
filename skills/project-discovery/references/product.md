# Product Discovery

Read the target project's `docs/product.md` if it exists; otherwise use `assets/product.md`. Ask the owner to describe the idea in their own words. Elicit only what remains unclear:

- Who actually uses it, what they are trying to accomplish, and what currently gets in their way.
- The primary scenarios from trigger to outcome, including the target platform and usage context.
- The smallest useful release: must-have capabilities, useful later ideas, and explicit exclusions.
- How the owner will recognize success, known constraints, and assumptions that still need validation.

Use concrete examples to distinguish a user goal from a feature list. Ask follow-ups when a proposed must-have does not support the stated goal or when a scenario lacks an outcome. Do not invent metrics or platform requirements.

Write `docs/product.md` in the target project. Preserve confirmed content, remove unused template prompts, and label unresolved decisions in Open Questions. Check that the summary, users, scenarios, scope, exclusions, and success measures agree. Then continue to requirements when the release boundary is clear enough.
